## What really is Virtual Machines?

for me Virtual machine is an isolated operating-system environment used to reproduce the platform, tools, configuration, and behavior expected by an application without changing or depending entirely on the host operating system.

## What's the purpose of Virtual Machines?

Its main purpose is not merely to “run another operating system.” Its purpose is to create a controlled development environment.
